Quick guide

Try this module

  1. Enter the 15 predictors or select Batch Upload (CSV) and upload the example file. The first column must contain unique sample IDs.
  2. Choose median, tertile or custom risk stratification. For custom cutoffs, enter comma-separated numeric thresholds, then run the prediction.
  3. Inspect risk scores and group assignments and download the CSV. A risk score is not a calibrated probability of an event at a specified time.

Model context. Saved surv.ranger model · 15 predictors · median, tertile and custom cutoffs. The demo uses the saved tutorial objects; it does not retrain models during a browser session.
